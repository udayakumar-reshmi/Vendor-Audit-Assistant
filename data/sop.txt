VENDOR COMPLIANCE SOP (SAMPLE)

1. Statutory Licences
1.1 The vendor must hold a valid Trade Licence. Expired licences are a Critical non-compliance.
1.2 The vendor must hold a valid Fire Safety Certificate for each operating location.
1.3 The vendor must hold a valid FSSAI licence if food is handled.

2. Payroll Compliance
2.1 PF and ESIC contributions must be paid by the 15th of every month.
2.2 Salary must be paid by the 7th of every month. Delays are a Major non-compliance.

3. Vehicle and Driver Documents
3.1 Every vehicle must have valid insurance and a valid fitness certificate.
3.2 Every driver must hold a valid driving licence and a completed background check.

4. Scoring
Critical = 10 points lost, Major = 5 points lost, Minor = 2 points lost.
Starting score is 100.
